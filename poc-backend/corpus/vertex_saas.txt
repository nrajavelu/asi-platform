Vertex SaaS — Enterprise Expansion Case (illustrative sample corpus, not a real filing)

Vertex SaaS reported net revenue retention (NRR) of 96% for the most recent
fiscal year, below its stated target of 110%. The 10-K attributes the shortfall
to "elongated renewal cycles as enterprise customers apply increased scrutiny to
software spend amid tighter IT budgets," without quantifying how much of the gap
is due to delayed renewals versus outright non-renewals.

The company's AI-assisted workflow automation module, released one quarter ago,
is now active on 41% of accounts by seat count, according to the Q2 product usage
report. The report notes this is the fastest feature adoption curve in the
company's five-year product history, measured by percentage of accounts adopting
within one quarter of general availability.

An internal feasibility memo prepared for the board estimates that expanding into
the European Union would require GDPR-compliant regional data residency,
adding approximately $2.1 million in incremental annual infrastructure cost. The
memo's stated scope is limited to "data residency and processing infrastructure
for EU customer data" and does not address costs or requirements for any other
regulated market.

The memo includes one paragraph noting that "the infrastructure investment may
have applications beyond the EU expansion," without naming specific markets,
providing a cost-sharing estimate, or citing a regulatory basis for reuse in any
other jurisdiction.

Sales leadership's quarterly business review states that accounts using the
AI-assisted workflow module renew at a materially higher rate than accounts that
have not adopted it, based on an internal cohort comparison covering two
renewal cycles. The review does not disclose the size of the two cohorts being
compared.
